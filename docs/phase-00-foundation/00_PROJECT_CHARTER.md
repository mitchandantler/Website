# 1. Executive Summary

The Mitch & Antler Platform is a long-term digital platform designed to support every aspect of the Mitch & Antler Café business.

The project begins as a modern café website but is intentionally designed to evolve into a complete business management platform.

Rather than maintaining multiple disconnected systems, the platform will provide a single, integrated solution for customer engagement, staff operations, reporting, and future business growth.

The system will be modular so new capabilities can be introduced without redesigning the entire application.

---

# 2. Vision Statement

To build a modern, reliable, and scalable digital platform that becomes the central operating system for Mitch & Antler Café.

The platform should improve customer experience, simplify business operations, and provide accurate business insights while remaining easy to maintain and extend.

---

# 3. Mission Statement

Create a platform that enables the business owner to manage the café from a single system while providing customers with a fast, informative, and enjoyable online experience.

---

---

# 12. Project Constraints

The project should:

* Maintain one primary Django codebase.
* Prefer Python wherever practical.
* Avoid unnecessary JavaScript frameworks.
* Be deployable to a Linux VPS.
* Remain affordable to host.
* Support future business growth without major redesign.

---

# 13. Risks

Potential risks include:

* Scope expansion
* Third-party API changes
* Hosting changes
* Security vulnerabilities
* Documentation becoming outdated
* Performance degradation as features grow

Risk mitigation should be reviewed at each phase.

# 15. Long-Term Vision

The Mitch & Antler Platform should become the digital backbone of the business.

The website is only the first step.

Over time, the same platform will support customer engagement, business operations, reporting, and future innovation through a single, consistent architecture.

The platform should remain flexible enough to support additional business locations, new technologies, and changing operational requirements without requiring a complete rebuild.
